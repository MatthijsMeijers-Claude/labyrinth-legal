Privacy Policy

The Labyrinth. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Who we are

The Labyrinth is an automated news project about artificial intelligence. One person in the Netherlands runs it. In this policy, "we" means that person.

What the app does

The Labyrinth has a small app that connects to TikTok through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. The app has one use. The owner of The Labyrinth's TikTok account logs in, and the app posts The Labyrinth's videos to that same account. The app is not offered to other TikTok users.

What data the app handles

When the account owner logs in, TikTok sends the app an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and the list of granted permissions. The app stores these on our own computer, in a file that only its owner can read. The tokens let the app post videos to The Labyrinth's account.

Before a post, the app asks TikTok for the account's posting settings, such as the allowed privacy levels and the maximum video length. It uses them only to check that the post can go ahead and does not store them.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers. It does not use cookies or tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data. The tokens are sent only to TikTok, as needed to post videos.

How long we keep it

We keep the tokens while the app is in use. You can remove the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings. You can also ask us to delete the stored tokens, and we will delete them.
